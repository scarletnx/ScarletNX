The loader of GPU driver packages on Android, src/nx/runtime/platform/gpu_driver_android.c and gpu_driver_hook.c,
is written after libadrenotools (src/driver.cpp, src/hook/hook_impl.cpp, src/hook/main_hook.c) and its
liblinkernsbypass (android_linker_ns.cpp, elf_soname_patcher.cpp): the linker namespaces a second copy of Android's
Vulkan loader and a package's driver load in, and the library that takes that copy's two driver-opening calls.
https://github.com/bylaws/libadrenotools
https://github.com/bylaws/liblinkernsbypass
