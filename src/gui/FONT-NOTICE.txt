scarletnx-gui uses the public-domain 8x16 VGA BIOS font from SeaBIOS.

Source: vgasrc/vgafonts.c, vgafont16
Commit: 81ec9ec0bcf45df11fb7f98339ec9036b546fca0
https://github.com/coreboot/seabios/blob/81ec9ec0bcf45df11fb7f98339ec9036b546fca0/vgasrc/vgafonts.c

Original notice:
These fonts come from ftp://ftp.simtel.net/pub/simtelnet/msdos/screen/fntcol16.zip
The package is (c) by Joseph Gil
The individual fonts are public domain

The integration and CP437 mapping are part of ScarletNX (GPL-3.0-or-later).
The font bitmaps retain their public-domain status.

The Latin letters past CP437 (those of U+00A0 to U+024F that CP437 lacks, src/gui/unifont-latin.hex) are the 8x16
glyphs of GNU Unifont 18.0.01, by Roman Czyborra, Paul Hardy and the Unifont contributors, kept as they are in that
file. The build fits them to the VGA font (scripts/build-glyphs.py, fit): each stem two columns wide, and each letter
raised two rows to the VGA font's baseline, an accented capital shortened by a row or two as the VGA font's own are.

Source: https://unifoundry.com/pub/unifont/unifont-18.0.01/font-builds/unifont-18.0.01.hex.gz
SHA-256: e66385c79a0b8b24a466f3129930e08a966a935b4bf3b28c6bb17a9df9bf791d, signed by Paul Hardy
https://unifoundry.com/unifont/

The wide letters the menu's languages show past CP437 and the Latin letters (src/gui/unifont-wide.hex: Chinese, as
po/*.po and tests/po/*.po use them) are the 16x16 glyphs of the same GNU Unifont 18.0.01, kept and compiled in as
they are in that file, unaltered.

Source: https://ftp.gnu.org/gnu/unifont/unifont-18.0.01/unifont_all-18.0.01.hex.gz

shares with unifont-18.0.01.hex above is the same
